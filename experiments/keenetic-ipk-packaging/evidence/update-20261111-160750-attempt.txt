# KN-1012 update attempt, 11 November 2026

## Question and environment

Could the rebuilt package complete postinst on the KN-1012 after increasing the
init readiness window to match the supervisor boot-recovery bound? Device:
Keenetic Giga KN-1012, KeeneticOS 5.2 Beta 3, Entware `aarch64-3.10`.

- Root-only backup from the preceding attempt and rollback IPK were present;
  rollback IPK hash matched `7351ecd795ec1f655761c14ffa8579f794a17f90e90d404c4f840182a7988053`.

## Observed result

`opkg install` passed target preflight. The supervisor started, restored the
committed AWG runtime, published readiness, and launched the panel. The package
then failed in the expected OpkgTun migration at
`candidate-backend-check`: `backend-validator timed out after 2m1.532s`. The
configured backend validation bound was two minutes, while the command limit
was five minutes. The migration reported failure, stopped Hestia services, and
left package status `install user unpacked` with the package-maintenance marker
present. No blind `opkg configure` or rollback was run.

At the post-failure read-only check, main IPv4 and IPv6 route snapshots still
matched the root-only pre-install snapshots. Policy rule snapshots differed by
the two Hestia guard rules associated with mark `0xffffaac`; these were observed
after supervisor startup/shutdown and have not been manually removed. Exact
rules and private config are retained only on the router backup, not copied
into this report. The hestia-cli server remained available.

## Conclusion and limits

The longer init wait fixed the first failure: the supervisor reached readiness.
The next blocker is the inner candidate backend-validation timeout, conclusively
identified by its fixed two-minute duration. The backend timeout and enclosing
Keenetic-ingress command timeout have now been raised to bounded ten- and
fifteen-minute windows respectively. A rebuilt package and another controlled
installation are still required to verify that this second fix completes the
migration. Client traffic, policy behavior, and rollback have not been tested.
